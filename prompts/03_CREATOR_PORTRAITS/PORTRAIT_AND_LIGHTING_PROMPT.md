# CREATOR PORTRAIT & STUDIO LIGHTING PROMPT

## Objective
Generate or stylize a creator portrait with studio-grade cinematic lighting, strong edge separation (rim light), and authentic facial expressions that blend seamlessly into any thumbnail style.

---

## The Prompt (Copy & Paste)

```markdown
You are a commercial portrait photographer and visual compositor.

I am supplying a photo of myself (or describing a creator portrait). I need to generate/stylize a clean, high-impact 16:9 thumbnail portrait that will be composited into a YouTube cover.

### Portrait Direction:
1. LIGHTING SETUP: 
   - Dramatic key light with soft shadow falloff.
   - Distinct colored rim/edge lighting (backlight) along shoulders and hair to ensure sharp separation from the background.
   - Clean catchlights in the eyes to convey energy and focus.
2. FACIAL EXPRESSION:
   - Authentic, engaged, intentional expression (intense focus, thoughtful concern, skeptical smirk, or revelation).
   - STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: Fake YouTube "gasping mouth open" shock face, overly forced smile, or blank passport stare.
3. COMPOSITION:
   - Positioned on either the left or right third of the 16:9 frame (Rule of Thirds).
   - Never center unless the style specifically calls for a symmetrical monolith.
   - Leave ample negative space on the opposite side for typography and scene storytelling.
4. INTEGRATION:
   - Match the overall color grading, grain texture, and stylistic treatment of the chosen reference pack.

Style reference to harmonize with:
[ATTACH OR SPECIFY STYLE REFERENCE IMAGE OR PACK NUMBER]
```
