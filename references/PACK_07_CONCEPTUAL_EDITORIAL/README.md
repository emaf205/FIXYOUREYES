# PACK 07 — CONCEPTUAL EDITORIAL

Eight free, individually downloadable **thumbnail style references** for AI YouTube Thumbnail Studio. All images feature embedded English demo headlines and explore a fictional AI/business topic. They are references for visual direction, not facts about your video.

## How to use

1. Copy the universal `MASTER_PROMPT.md` (distributed separately).
2. Upload one or more full transcripts (YouTube video, lesson, or podcast).
3. Upload one or more PNG references from this pack, or your own references.
4. Optionally add your portrait and corresponding video URLs.
5. State how many **separate thumbnails per transcript** you want. For example, 4 transcripts × 2 variants = 8 images.

The reference guides palette, texture, typography, composition energy, and art direction. It is **not** a template to copy. Text in generated thumbnails must match the **language of each transcript**, even when the English words in a reference differ. Each variation should have a distinct concept, not simply a recolor.
