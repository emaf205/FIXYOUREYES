# PACK 02 — DESIGN MOVEMENTS

Eight free visual references for **AI YouTube Thumbnail Studio**. All eight depict the same fictional seven-day AI business experiment, making it easy to compare artistic styles.

## Contents

- `ATLAS_PACK_02_DESIGN_MOVEMENTS.png` — overview of the eight references.
- `references/` — eight full-resolution, individual PNG images.

## Styles

1. Bauhaus — geometric shapes, primary colors, functional typography.
2. Brutalism — oversized type, raw contrast, stark framing.
3. Swiss Style — disciplined grid and typographic hierarchy.
4. Constructivism — diagonals, dynamic geometry, poster composition.
5. Art Deco — metallic geometry, elegant symmetry, ornamental detail.
6. Memphis — playful color, patterns, and geometric contrast.
7. De Stijl — primary-color planes and strong black rules.
8. New Wave — expressive typography and energetic layered graphics.

## Quick start

1. Copy the universal `MASTER_PROMPT.md` (distributed separately).
2. Upload one or more transcripts (video, lesson, or podcast).
3. Upload one or more references from this pack, or provide your own.
4. Optionally upload your portrait and video URLs.
5. Indicate how many thumbnails you want **per transcript**. Four transcripts × two variants = eight final images.

References define visual language, **not** the scene, wording or exact composition. Thumbnail text follows the transcript's language. The demonstration content and any statistics printed on these sample images are fictional and are not claims about real-world outcomes.
