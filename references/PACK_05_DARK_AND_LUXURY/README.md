## Use

1. Open the universal `MASTER_PROMPT.md` from AI YouTube Thumbnail Studio.
2. Attach one or more full video, lesson, or podcast transcripts (recommended).
3. Attach one or more individual references from this pack. The Atlas is for browsing, not for exact style targeting.
4. Optionally attach a creator portrait or relevant video URL.
5. Specify how many final thumbnails you want **per transcript** (e.g. 4 transcripts × 2 = 8 images).

The reference guides style, **not the scene or exact composition**. Thumbnail copy follows the language of its transcript. Every output should have its own contextual concept, and no demo performance figures should be treated as facts.
